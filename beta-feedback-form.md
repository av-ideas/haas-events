# Professional Events Tracker: beta feedback

Thanks for testing the tracker. This takes about 3 minutes. Answers are anonymous unless you add your email at the end.

---

**1. How easy was it to sign in?** *(Scale 1–5: 1 = couldn't sign in, 5 = effortless)*
If you picked 1 or 2, what happened? *(Short answer)*

**2. How relevant were the listed events to you?** *(Scale 1–5: 1 = not relevant, 5 = very relevant)*

**3. Did you find an event you hadn't heard about anywhere else?** *(Multiple choice)*
- Yes, and I plan to attend
- Yes, but I won't attend
- No

**4. Which kinds of events do you want more of?** *(Checkboxes, pick up to 3)*
- Career and recruiting
- Speakers and panels
- Club events
- Socials
- Startups and VC
- Finance and investing
- Conferences
- Professional networking
- Community and service

**5. Which features did you use?** *(Checkboxes)*
- Filters
- Search
- 14-day strip at the top
- "I'm going"
- .ics calendar file
- Google Calendar link
- Export .ics (all events in view)
- Event page / registration link
- Report a problem
- None of these

**6. Are you comfortable with cohort mates seeing which events you mark "I'm going"?** *(Multiple choice)*
- Yes
- Yes, but I'd like a private option
- No, so I won't use it

**7. Did anything look broken or wrong?** *(Checkboxes)*
- An event's date, time, or place
- A link that didn't work or went to the wrong page
- Layout on my phone
- Filters or search results
- Nothing looked wrong

Tell us where. *(Short answer, optional)*

**8. How often would you check the tracker?** *(Multiple choice)*
- Daily
- A few times a week
- Weekly
- Less than weekly
- I wouldn't use it

**9. What one change would make you use it more?** *(Paragraph)*

**10. How likely are you to recommend the tracker to a cohort mate?** *(Scale 0–10)*
