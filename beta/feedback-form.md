# Beta tester feedback form

Ten questions, about 3 minutes. Built to paste into Google Forms (question type in brackets).
Suggested form description: "Thanks for testing Professional Events Tracker. Your answers shape what gets fixed and
built next. This is an independent student project, not a UC Berkeley or Haas survey."

1. **How often did you open the tracker during the beta?** [Multiple choice]
   Daily · A few times a week · About once a week · Once or twice · Not at all

2. **How easy was it to sign in with your Berkeley account?** [Linear scale 1–5]
   1 = I couldn't get in · 5 = Effortless

3. **How useful is the tracker for finding events worth your time?** [Linear scale 1–5]
   1 = Not useful · 5 = Very useful

4. **Did the tracker show you an event you wouldn't have found otherwise?** [Multiple choice]
   Yes, and I went · Yes, but I didn't go · No

5. **Which event sources are most valuable to you?** [Checkboxes]
   Bear Necessities · Other Haas newsletters (EW Wire, CMG) · Club events (Campus Groups) · Haas Alumni events ·
   Bay Area industry events (tech, finance, conferences)

6. **How accurate were event details (date, time, place, link)?** [Linear scale 1–5]
   1 = Often wrong · 5 = Always right

7. **Which features did you use?** [Checkboxes]
   Filters · Search · 14-day strip · "I'm going" · Seeing who's going · .ics download · Google Calendar link ·
   Export .ics · Report a bug

8. **Does seeing which cohort mates are going affect whether you attend?** [Multiple choice]
   A lot · Somewhat · Not at all · I didn't notice it

9. **What's missing, confusing, or frustrating?** [Paragraph]
   Include any events or sources you expected to see.

10. **How likely are you to recommend the tracker to another EWMBA classmate?** [Linear scale 0–10]
    0 = Not at all likely · 10 = Extremely likely
