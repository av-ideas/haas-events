# Professional Events Tracker: beta feedback form

Ten questions, about 3 minutes. Paste into Google Forms; the question type is in italics.
Form description: "Thanks for testing Professional Events Tracker. Answers are anonymous unless you add your email
at the end. This is an independent student project, not a UC Berkeley or Haas survey."

---

**1. How often did you check the tracker during the beta?** *(Multiple choice)*
Daily · A few times a week · Weekly · Less than weekly · Not at all

**2. How easy was it to sign in with your Berkeley account?** *(Linear scale 1–5: 1 = couldn't sign in, 5 = effortless)*
If you picked 1 or 2, what happened? *(Short answer, optional)*

**3. How relevant were the listed events to you?** *(Linear scale 1–5: 1 = not relevant, 5 = very relevant)*

**4. Did you find an event you hadn't heard about anywhere else?** *(Multiple choice)*
Yes, and I went or plan to · Yes, but I won't go · No

**5. Which kinds of events do you want more of?** *(Checkboxes, up to 3)*
Career and recruiting · Speakers and panels · Club events · Alumni networking · Startups and VC ·
Finance and investing · Industry conferences · Socials · Community and service

**6. Which features did you use?** *(Checkboxes)*
Filters · Search · 14-day strip · "I'm going" · Seeing who's going · .ics file · Google Calendar link ·
Export .ics · Event registration link · Report a problem · None

**7. Did anything look broken or wrong?** *(Checkboxes)*
An event's date, time, or place · A link that failed or went to the wrong page · Layout on my phone ·
Filters or search results · Sign-in · Nothing looked wrong
Where? *(Short answer, optional)*

**8. Are you comfortable with cohort mates seeing the events you mark "I'm going"?** *(Multiple choice)*
Yes · Yes, but I'd like a private option · No, so I won't use it

**9. What one change would make you use the tracker more?** *(Paragraph)*

**10. How likely are you to recommend the tracker to a cohort mate?** *(Linear scale 0–10)*

---

*Optional:* Your email, if you're open to a 10-minute follow-up chat. *(Short answer)*
